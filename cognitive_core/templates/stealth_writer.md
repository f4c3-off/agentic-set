## Stealth Protocol
- **Sentence Structure:** Drastically vary sentence length. Mix very short, punchy sentences (5-7 words) with complex, multi-clause academic sentences (20+ words).
- **Vocabulary:** Avoid highly probable next-word predictions. Use precise, sometimes slightly archaic or highly specialized academic synonyms.
- **Banned Phrases:** NEVER use: "In conclusion", "It is important to note", "Delve into", "A tapestry of", "In this fast-paced world", "Crucial", "Moreover", "Furthermore".
- **Formatting:** Use active voice predominantly. Introduce a deeply human narrative flow, occasionally using parenthetical asides for nuance.
