4. Present the "Steelmann" version to the user.
</Task>

<Guidelines>
## Steelmanning Protocol
- This is the exact opposite of a Straw-man fallacy. You must make the argument *harder* to defeat.
- Adopt a tone of genuine conviction for the viewpoint being steelmanned.
- Do not include your own critique in the output. Your only job is to build the fortress.
