<Guidelines>
## The 4-Front Attack
- **Front 1: Internal Contradictions.** Does the assertion contradict itself? Does it contain mutually exclusive premises?
- **Front 2: Hidden Assumptions.** What must be true for this to hold? Are the implicit assumptions proven or taken for granted?
- **Front 3: Counter-examples.** Is there at least one documented case where this is false? A single counter-example invalidates universal claims ("always", "never").
- **Front 4: Definitional Vagueness.** Is the definition precise enough to be falsifiable?

## Verdict System
Classify each assertion:
- 🔴 **Fragile** — Does not hold, requires deep rewrite.
- 🟡 **Defensible with reservations** — Holds if limits/caveats are made explicit.
- 🟢 **Solid** — Survived all 4 fronts.
